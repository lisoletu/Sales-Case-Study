# 📊 Sales Analysis Case Study

## Project Overview

This project is a data analytics case study focused on analysing historical sales data to generate insights into revenue, pricing, profitability, sales volume, and promotional performance.

The analysis was conducted to understand sales patterns, evaluate product pricing behaviour, measure gross profit, and assess how changes in price influenced quantity demanded.

## Business Objectives

The analysis aimed to answer the following key questions:

- What is the daily sales price per unit?
- What is the average unit sales price?
- What is the daily gross profit percentage?
- What is the daily gross profit per unit?
- How does the product perform during promotional periods?
- What is the Price Elasticity of Demand during selected promotional periods?
- What additional sales and profitability insights can be identified from the data?
